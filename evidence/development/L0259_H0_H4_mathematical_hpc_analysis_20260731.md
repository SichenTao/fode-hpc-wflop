# L0259 H0-H4 mathematical and HPC analysis

## H0 — paper-paired scope

The target contribution is SUGGA, not the paper's CGA or AGA comparison
baselines. Its native problem family is the full factorial product of three
wind profiles, thirteen landowner-participation masks, and three fixed
turbine counts: 117 optimization problems. Each problem receives an
independently trained 10,000-layout SVR artifact and 100 independent
120-population, 200-generation SUGGA runs.

## H1 — authority and conflict resolution

The hashed primary PDF and its directly linked MIT author repository are
both available. The paper is primary for physical statements: 154 m cells,
88 m hub height and ordinary probability rates. The source completes the
otherwise underspecified executable GA and gives all L1-L12 arrays, the
120-population setting, SVR parameters and five-candidate relocation.

The source's 231 m cells, 80 m hub, normal-threshold draws, ignored
crossover rate and wall-time reseeding are not silently merged into the
paper identity. `paper_probability` is formal primary;
`source_normal_threshold` remains executable and carries those source
behaviors.

## H2 — objective and evaluator factorization

For wind state \(s\), downstream candidate node \(i\), and upstream node
\(j\), all geometry is invariant across optimization:

\[
q_{sij} =
\left[
\frac{2}{3}
\cdot\frac{r^2}{(r+\alpha\Delta y_{sij})^2}
\cdot\frac{A_{\cap}(s,i,j)}{\pi r^2}
\right]^2
\]

when \(j\) is upstream of \(i\), and zero otherwise. Therefore all
trigonometry, ordering, wake expansion and circle-overlap operations are
precomputed once into a state-node-node tensor. A layout evaluation only
indexes \(q_{sij}\), reduces squared deficits, applies the GE-1.5sle curve,
and takes the stated wind-probability expectation. This preserves the
paper/source equations while removing repeated geometry from every
physical evaluation.

## H3 — surrogate training

Each Monte-Carlo layout is independent and is generated by uniform
fixed-cardinality selection over the case's available cells. Its truth
evaluation is independent, so all 10,000 samples are mapped over the
persistent CPU team. Cell-conditioned mean turbine power produces the
paper's 144 response values; unavailable and never occupied cells retain
the source value zero.

The author Python calls `sklearn.svm.SVR`. The reproduction vendors the
official LIBSVM C++ solver used by sklearn and trains the exact stated RBF
epsilon-SVR configuration: \(C=2000\), \(\gamma=0.3\), and
\(\epsilon=0.1\). The trained 144-node prediction vector is immutable and
reused across the problem's 100 optimization repeats.

## H4 — optimization and resource mapping

Population truth evaluations dominate each generation and are mapped over
one persistent all-core executor. Worst-turbine relocation and the
crossover/mutation construction of children are also independent by
individual and use the same team. Ranking and parent-index assembly remain
small deterministic reductions. Counter-keyed random events make the
scientific trajectory invariant to worker scheduling.

H6 compares the same C++ source, case, seed, 34,000 physical evaluations
and scientific hash with one versus all twenty Waffle workers. Admission
requires speedup in Monte-Carlo truth evaluation, population truth
evaluation and end-to-end time. Formal execution then uses only the
all-twenty-core configuration, writes every run receipt, and totals
11,700 runs and 282 million complete layout evaluations (including 117
one-time 10,000-layout surrogate artifacts).
